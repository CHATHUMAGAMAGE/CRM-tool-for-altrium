# User Personas

## Project
**Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System**

**Client:** Altrium (Software Company)

---

# Overview

The CRM system is designed for a software company that manages customer relationships, sales opportunities, software projects, and communication between different departments.

The following personas represent the primary users who will interact with the system.

---

# Persona 1 – Sales Representative

## Profile

| Attribute | Details |
|----------|----------|
| Name | Nuwan Perera |
| Age | 28 |
| Education | Bachelor's Degree in Marketing |
| Status | Single |
| Occupation | Sales Representative |
| Location | Colombo, Sri Lanka |
| Tech Literacy | High |

---

## Bio

Nuwan is responsible for generating new business opportunities and maintaining relationships with potential and existing customers. He spends most of his day communicating with clients through emails, phone calls, WhatsApp, and meetings. Since he frequently works outside the office, he needs quick access to customer information from any device.

---

## Core Needs

- Access all customer information in one place.
- Create and update customer records.
- Record customer meetings, calls and emails.
- Schedule follow-up reminders.
- Track customer deals throughout the sales process.

---

## Frustrations

- Customer information is scattered across different platforms.
- Misses follow-up opportunities.
- Previous conversations are difficult to locate.
- Duplicate customer information.
- Lost sales due to poor tracking.

---

## User Stories

- As a Sales Representative, I want to create new leads so that potential customers are recorded.
- As a Sales Representative, I want to update customer information so that records remain accurate.
- As a Sales Representative, I want to log customer calls, emails, meetings and notes so that every interaction is recorded.
- As a Sales Representative, I want to schedule follow-up reminders so that I never miss contacting a customer.
- As a Sales Representative, I want to update the status of a deal so that everyone knows its current stage.
