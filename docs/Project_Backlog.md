# Product Backlog

## Project
Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System

Client: Altrium

---

# Product Vision

Develop a centralized CRM system that enables Altrium to manage customers, leads, sales opportunities, software projects and customer communication in one place.

# Sprint Summary


## Sprint 1 (Core CRM)

- Authentication
- User Management
- Dashboard
- Customer Management
- Lead Management
- Deal Creation

Goal:
Develop the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) that allows users to securely log in, manage customers, leads and deals.

---

## Sprint 2 (Enhanced CRM)

- Communication History
- Follow-up Management
- Project Management
- Software Development Module
- Reports
- Executive Dashboard
- Notifications
- Audit Logs

Goal:
Enhance the CRM by adding project management, reporting, collaboration and administrative features.
